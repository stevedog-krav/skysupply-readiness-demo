# Public workflow references

Reviewed November 16, 2026. These primary sources inform terminology, service categories and workflow design. They do not supply the prototype's inventory, aircraft schedules, supplier quotes, prices, model accuracy or shipping rates.

| Source | Relevant design implication |
| --- | --- |
| [ILS parts RFQ workflow](https://static.ilsmart.com/help/members.ilsmart.com/NetHelp/createpartsrfq.htm) | Select recipients for an exact part, specify quantity and condition, and review the RFQ before release. NE and SV terminology informs the condition selector. A condition code alone does not establish eligibility or documentation completeness. |
| [SkySelect product](https://www.skyselect.com/product) | Demand-to-sourcing, purchasing, supplier response and delivery tracking are established workflows. Our interface should not imply that the broad combination is new. |
| [Honeywell asset availability](https://www.honeywellaerospace.com/us/en/solutions/commercial/capabilities/asset-availability) | Engine/APU rentals, exchange and temporary asset support are distinct alternatives to a replacement purchase. No Honeywell program, quote or participation is represented in our records. |
| [Honeywell rental policy](https://aerospace.honeywell.com/content/dam/aerobt/en/documents/learn/services/asset-availability/user-documents/hon-aero-2026-engine-rental-pricing-and-policy-en.pdf) | Actual rental terms vary, and the document describes monthly billing for specified air-transport/regional APUs. The app's editable daily-rate example is an invented scenario, not a universal industry rate or Honeywell quotation. |
| [UPS urgent freight](https://www.ups.com/us/en/supplychain/freight/urgent-services) | Time-critical freight can involve next-flight-out, dedicated surface, charter or hand-carry arrangements; do not treat an APU as an ordinary parcel or guarantee a route. |
| [FedEx aerospace critical services](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/aerospace/critical-time-sensitive.html) | Large or time-critical shipments can require special handling and charter arrangements. Packaging and service feasibility must be confirmed before booking. |
| [Amazon Shipping overview](https://shipping.amazon.com/) | Amazon Shipping is a ground-parcel service, distinct from Amazon retail/Prime benefits. It is not presented as an AOG or engine-freight service. |
| [Amazon Shipping requirements](https://shipping.amazon.com/help) | Account, pickup/delivery coverage, package weight/dimensions and permissible-goods restrictions require verification. The app only shows a conditional standard-parcel estimate, without asserting eligibility. |
| [DHL aerospace logistics](https://www.dhl.com/us-en/home/global-forwarding/industry-expertise/sectors_aerospace_and_aviation.html) | Specialist aerospace logistics uses AOG response, coordinated routing and milestone visibility, informing the shipment progress presentation. |

All transport figures remain deterministic illustrative estimates. No rate API, supplier portal, carrier account, RFQ communication or shipment booking is connected. The selected provider/service and estimate are carried through the approval workflow to keep calculations consistent.
